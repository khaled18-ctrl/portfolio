AI Career Prediction

A machine learning web app that takes a user's background and predicts the career paths best suited to them — built and shipped end to end, from a raw dataset to a link anyone can open.

Problem
People exploring a career change or a first job often don't know which paths actually fit their profile — the choice is guesswork.
Approach
Cleaned and prepared the training data with pandas and NumPy, then built and evaluated a prediction model with scikit-learn — covering feature engineering, classification, and validation.
Deployment
Shipped as a live, interactive Streamlit app — no setup required, the model runs the moment a user opens the link.
My role
End to end and solo: data preparation, model development, and deployment, with the source tracked on GitHub.
